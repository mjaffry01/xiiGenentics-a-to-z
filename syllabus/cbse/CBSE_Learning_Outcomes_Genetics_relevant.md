## Broad Class 12 Biology learning outcomes that explicitly name genetics ideas

From the Class 12 LO list in the same PDF (examples that mention genetics topics):

- Differentiates organisms, phenomena and processes… e.g. **transcription and translation**; **genotype and phenotype**; etc.
- Applies scientific terminology… e.g. **mutation; pleiotropy; sex determination; syndrome**; etc.
- Explains processes… e.g. **mendelian and chromosomal disorders**, **human genome project**, **use of DNA fingerprinting in forensic science**, etc.
- Describes contribution of scientists… e.g. **Mendelian genetics to Morgan’s work for linkage and recombination**, **Hershey and Martha Chase’s experiment** establishing DNA as genetic material, **Watson and Crick model of DNA**, etc.
- Makes linkages with other disciplines… e.g. **mathematical models of monohybrid and dihybrid cross; pedigree analysis; molecular basis of DNA and RNA**, etc.

## Chapter 4 — Principles of Inheritance and Variation (content-domain outcomes / indicators)

Key concepts and associated indicators extracted from the framework tables:

| Key concept | Example content-domain outcomes / indicators |
| --- | --- |
| Mendel’s laws / Inheritance | Analyze Mendel’s pea-plant observations; schematically illustrate monohybrid, dihybrid, and test crosses with Punnett squares; evaluate genotype and phenotype; design/conduct monohybrid and dihybrid inheritance experiments |
| Inheritance of two genes / linkage | Explain chromosomal theory of inheritance; describe linkage and recombination using a cross; calculate progeny percentages for linkage/recombination patterns |
| Polygenic traits | Explain polygenic traits and additive allele effects; interpret population data for polygenic traits |
| Pleiotropy | Explain how a single gene can show multiple phenotypic expressions |
| Sex determination | Differentiate sex-determination mechanisms in humans, birds, and honey bees; outline Henking’s observations; apply concepts to social myths about sex of child |
| Mutations | Explain mutation and its role in inheritance |
| Genetic disorders | Explain causes of genetic disorders; examine pedigree charts; compare Mendelian vs chromosomal disorders; differentiate Klinefelter’s and Turner’s syndromes; use pedigree for Mendelian disorder transmission |

## Chapter 5 — Molecular Basis of Inheritance (content-domain outcomes / indicators)

| Key concept | Example content-domain outcomes / indicators |
| --- | --- |
| The DNA | Draw/explain DNA structure; differentiate DNA vs RNA; euchromatin vs heterochromatin; compare base-pair amounts across organisms |
| Search for genetic material | Describe experiments/inferences of Griffith; Avery, MacLeod & McCarty; Hershey & Chase |
| Replication | Describe semi-conservative replication and step-by-step mechanism (incl. Meselson & Stahl contribution context) |
| Transcription | Illustrate a transcription unit and mRNA formation; differentiate prokaryotic vs eukaryotic transcription (RNA polymerases / mRNA) |
| Genetic code | State salient features; convert between nucleotide and amino-acid sequences using codon table; relate 64 codons via permutation/combination reasoning |
| Translation | Explain/illustrate tRNA structure and step-by-step translation |
| Regulation of gene expression | Describe lac operon mechanism and importance of gene regulation |
| Human Genome Project | Summarize methodology and major findings |
| DNA fingerprinting | Describe principles/process of DNA fingerprinting |
