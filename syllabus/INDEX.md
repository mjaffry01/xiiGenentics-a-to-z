## Genetics topics actually covered (major headings only)

### CBSE Class 12 — Unit VII Genetics and Evolution (20 marks)

1. **Chapter-4: Principles of Inheritance and Variation** — Mendelian inheritance; deviations (incomplete dominance, co-dominance, multiple alleles/blood groups, pleiotropy); polygenic inheritance (elementary); chromosome theory; chromosomes & genes; sex determination (humans, birds, honey bee); linkage & crossing over; sex-linked inheritance (haemophilia, colour blindness); Mendelian disorders (thalassemia); chromosomal disorders (Down’s, Turner’s, Klinefelter’s).
2. **Chapter-5: Molecular Basis of Inheritance** — search for genetic material / DNA as genetic material; DNA & RNA structure; DNA packaging; replication; Central Dogma; transcription; genetic code; translation; gene expression & regulation (lac operon); genome / Human & rice genome projects; DNA fingerprinting.
3. **Related practicals:** DNA isolation; meiosis spotting; Mendelian inheritance with seeds; pedigree charts.

### NCERT Class 12 Biology — Unit VII

1. **Ch. 4 Principles of Inheritance and Variation:** 4.1 Mendel’s Laws; 4.2 One gene; 4.3 Two genes; 4.4 Sex determination; 4.5 Mutation; 4.6 Genetic disorders.
2. **Ch. 5 Molecular Basis of Inheritance:** 5.1 The DNA; 5.2 Search for genetic material; 5.3 RNA world; 5.4 Replication; 5.5 Transcription; 5.6 Genetic code; 5.7 Translation; 5.8 Regulation of gene expression; 5.9 Human Genome Project; 5.10 DNA fingerprinting.

### NEET (UG) — Biology Unit 7: Genetics and Evolution

1. **Heredity and variation** — aligned with CBSE Ch. 4 topic list (Mendelism, deviations, sex determination, linkage, sex-linked traits, Mendelian & chromosomal disorders).
2. **Molecular basis of Inheritance** — aligned with CBSE Ch. 5, plus explicit **protein biosynthesis**.
3. **Evolution** — same official unit (included in source PDFs; not the genetics-article focus).
